# Known issues in v0.5.0-rc.1

The [public RC](https://github.com/hyp36rmax/project-ot/releases/tag/v0.5.0-rc.1) is available. Solo, Local VS and LAN VS were physically validated during development, including 38/38 consolidated gameplay UAT and four endurance sessions exceeding 60 minutes. The final corrected RC full physical cycle was not repeated. The [validation history](validation-history.md) separates those accepted results from the specific remaining cases below. Experience source-scope and installation review remain separate.

## Controllers

Xbox Solo and the documented Xbox/HORI and Tanita/HORI Local VS combinations were physically accepted, alongside LAN ownership/focus. Profiles do not certify every device revision, adapter or wiring combination; specific final-RC reconnection/reassignment cases remain open. Reclaim ambiguous reconnected devices. An earlier Solo-to-Title navigation report was not reproduced on physical retest; no routing change was made. If it returns, record both player assignments and the claim order through [F12 Feedback](feedback.md).

## Display

Original 4:3, Hor+ 16:9, Single Display/Dual Display VS and monitor placement have physical development acceptance. Automatic and explicit effective aspects use the same Local VS Single Display geometry. Selected final-RC first-frame checks, monitor/DPI recovery and additional rendering-quality/hardware combinations remain open. Keep/Revert confirms a settings plan; start a new session for saved launch changes. Remaining horizontal damage-flash coverage checks at higher render heights are not closed by the layout correction or the passing native replay.

## Audio

Fresh-package defaults are 100% and unmuted; explicit saved preferences are preserved. Named endpoints and Separate Audio settings do not qualify independent live-cabinet routing. See [Audio](audio.md) and complete audible RC checks.

## LAN VS

LAN discovery was physically validated across seven different PC setups, with multiple completed Ethernet and Wi-Fi matches and extended linked sessions. Intermittent `0xB002`, a previously observed Wi-Fi dropout, reversed roles, repeated failure/reconnect recovery and permission-denial cases remain open. These results do not establish every pairing or universal reliability. An intentional Ethernet exit is not classified as a confirmed network failure. Wired Ethernet is recommended. [Direct Connect](lan-vs.md#direct-connect) does not bypass compatibility or permissions.

## Windows network authorization

Local VS loopback bindings are verified, but the producer and trigger of a reported Windows firewall dialog remain unknown. Do not disable protection or create broad exceptions as a workaround. Record the application name, executable path and play mode; see [network access](network-access.md).

## Menus, recovery and exit

Development Title/Pause, Alt+Tab and session-lifecycle acceptance is retained. The exact corrected RC still has selected device, monitor and cleanup/recovery combinations to qualify. Linked gameplay may continue behind the native menu. Current Global Exit can close other recognized Project OT copies in the same Windows session when Title Exit is confirmed; close other sessions normally before testing.

## Longer endurance and recovery coverage

Four accepted physical development sessions exceeded 60 minutes each. Separate 12-hour Solo/Local VS, monitor sleep/disconnection and sudden-power-loss qualification remain future work; completed hour-plus endurance is not being described as untested.

## Historical configuration checks

Two older configuration assertions remain in the retained reports, without a confirmed rerun or fix on the corrected RC. They are not verified current-package failures or silently resolved results. See the [historical configuration record](validation-history.md#historical-configuration-results).

## Features outside this candidate

Vertical expansion, Internet Netplay, rollback, spectators and gameplay-triggered rumble are not offered features. No benchmark or latency improvement is claimed for this RC.
